MATCHDAY GUIDE

Everything you need to know

Junior football · 5v5

Two games per week. 2 × 10-minute halves; a straight turnaround at half-time. Hooter signals start, half-time and full-time; restart promptly after changing ends.

  • Goalkeepers permitted; bring your own gloves if required.
  • Throw-ins, no offside, free kicks and penalties where appropriate.
  • Rolling substitutions permitted.

Senior mixed · 7v7

30-minute matches, two 15-minute halves. At least two female or under-15 players on the field at all times. Minimum five players to start.

No slide tackles, no offside, indirect free kicks, rolling substitutions. Only the goalkeeper may occupy the half-circle.
